Privacy Policy for Snack Stack

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Snack Stack ("the game"), a mobile game published by Martin Bogoje. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past — so the short version first.

The short version. The game has no accounts, no login and no servers of its own. Your scores, credits, settings and progress are stored only on your own device. The one thing that leaves your phone is advertising data, collected by Google AdMob so it can choose which ads to show you. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

1. Information the game itself collects

None that leaves your device. Snack Stack has no backend. It does not create an account, ask for your name or email, or transmit your gameplay anywhere. The following are written to your device's private app storage and stay there:

  • High scores and local leaderboards, per game mode
  • In-game credits, purchased items and score-multiplier level
  • Power-up counts and run boosts
  • Daily mission and daily challenge progress, and your play streak
  • Music and sound-effect volume settings
  • One suspended game board, if you choose to save a run and finish it later
  • Your position in the first-run tutorial

This data is removed when you uninstall the game.

Backup to your own Google account

The game has Android Auto Backup enabled. Android may copy the files listed above to your personal Google Drive so your progress survives a reinstall or a new phone. This is performed by the Android operating system, under your Google account and your control — the data goes to your Drive, not to us, and we cannot read it. You can turn this off in your device settings under Google Backup.

2. Information collected by third parties

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To do this, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information — model, operating system version, language, coarse region
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed or tapped

Google uses this to select and measure ads, limit repetition, and detect fraud. Google is an independent controller of this data. Their handling is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (purchases)

If you buy something in the game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment details are entered into Google's systems and are never seen by, or transmitted to, the game. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed, which is used to unlock the item on your device. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What we do not do

  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We have no server that receives your gameplay, scores or device data.
  • We do not ask for contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

4. Your choices

What you wantHow
Stop personalised ads Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, then reset or delete your advertising ID. Ads will still appear, but will not be personalised.
Remove ads entirely Buy the "Remove Ads" item in the in-game shop.
Delete everything the game stored Uninstall the game. To also remove the cloud copy, turn off or clear Google Backup in your device settings.
Stop backups to Drive Android: Settings → Google → Backup.

5. Children

Snack Stack is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will act on it.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Because the game holds no personal data on any server of ours, there is no account for us to export or erase — deleting the app removes everything it stored. For the advertising data described in section 2, Google acts as the data controller, and requests to access or delete it should be directed to Google using the links above. If you are in the EEA or UK, an ad consent prompt is presented where required, and you may change your choice at any time from the game's settings.

7. Data retention

Game data lives on your device until you delete it, and in your Google Drive backup for as long as your device settings keep it. Advertising data is retained by Google according to their own retention schedule.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage area, which Android isolates from other apps. Because no gameplay data is transmitted, there is no transfer for us to secure.
